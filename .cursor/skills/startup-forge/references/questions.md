# Questions (max 3 per stage)

Ask only if the answer changes the verdict or scaffold.

## High leverage

1. Who is the first 50 users by name/role, and where do they hang out?
2. Will they pay monthly, once, or only if free? What do they pay today instead?
3. What can you demo in 14 days that looks inevitable?
4. Consumer habit or B2B workflow — which do you want to live in for a year?
5. Any domain you refuse (health, kids, fintech, crypto)?
6. Is the goal YC-shaped company, cashflow micro-product, or both (venture + satellite)?

## Defaults if unanswered

- Solo consumer/prosumer product under Shua Labs
- Monthly subscription preferred; micro one-time OK in `micro` mode
- Demo in 14 days required
- Avoid heavy regulation unless user opts in

## Style

One message, numbered, easy to answer in 30 seconds. Then continue.
