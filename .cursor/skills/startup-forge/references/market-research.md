# Market research playbook

Use live tools (Context.dev `web-search`, `web-scrape-markdown`, GitHub search). Prefer primary sources.

## Required searches (validate / generate)

Run focused queries; scrape 1–3 best pages max.

1. **Category + year** — `"<category> market 2026"` OR growth stats
2. **Competitors** — `"<idea keywords>" app OR saas competitors`
3. **Complaints** — `site:reddit.com <problem>` or forum pain
4. **YC alignment** — current `ycombinator.com/rfs` themes vs idea
5. **Pricing comps** — competitor pricing pages

## Market Card template

```markdown
### Market Card
**Category:** ...
**Why now:** ... (cite)
**Demand proof:** ... (cite)
**Incumbents:**
- Name — what they own — pricing — URL
**Gaps / edge cases:** ...
**Crowd risk:** low | med | high
**Assumptions:** ...
```

## Edge-finding questions

- What do incumbents force users to do that feels broken?
- Where is AI cost now low enough that a consumer habit becomes viable?
- What can Josh ship that a big team would not bother to make tasteful?
- Is there a wedge audience that already congregates (Discord, subreddit, job title)?

## Anti-patterns

- Do not paste 2,000 words of scraped fluff
- Do not treat App Store screenshots as proof of retention
- Do not confuse "people talk about AI" with "people will pay for this"
- Prefer willingness-to-pay signals: existing subscriptions, freelancers hiring, paid communities
