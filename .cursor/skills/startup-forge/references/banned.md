# Banned (auto-reject or heavy rewrite)

If an idea matches these, Kill or force a non-obvious wedge.

## Product clichés

- ChatGPT wrapper with a thin UI
- "AI for X" with no workflow, no memory, no interactive loop
- Linear / Notion / Todoist / Asana clones
- Generic CRM, generic dashboard, generic note app
- Another link-in-bio, another habit tracker with streaks only
- Marketplace with two-sided cold start and no unfair seed audience
- Crypto token as the product (rails OK; token theater not)

## Fake validation

- "Everyone needs this"
- Invented TAM/SAM/SOM numbers
- Competitor lists with no URLs
- Scores without research
- Pitch decks before a core loop people will open daily/weekly

## Bad solo bets (default Kill)

- Heavy regulated healthcare/fintech unless wedge is clearly software-only and compliant path is short
- Hardware, robotics, defense manufacturing (unless Josh explicitly wants that lane)
- Services-heavy agencies disguised as SaaS
- Ideas that need a sales team in month one

## Coolness failures

- No demo moment in 30 seconds
- Looks like a homework assignment
- Only impressive if you already believe the pitch
- Would not put under Shua Labs brand

## Allowed exceptions

Rewrite is OK if you remove the cliché and keep a sharp interactive or agentic workflow people pay for.
