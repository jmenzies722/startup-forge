# Product research: Startup Forge as a business

## Verdict on packing this as a product

### Internal tool / Shua Labs OS — **GO**

This is the highest-ROI version. It compounds every venture you ship, plugs into Shua Department, and uses live research + scaffold (where IdeaProof / ValidatorAI / WorthBuild usually stop at a PDF score).

### Packaged SaaS "AI idea validator" alone — **NO-GO as YC lead**

The category is crowded (IdeaProof, ValidatorAI, DimeADozen, WorthBuild, Preuve, MiskMap, etc.). Buyers already complain most tools are ChatGPT wrappers with confidence. YC Fall 2026 wants consumer habits and real systems, not another founder-meta tool unless retention is insane.

### Hybrid that can work — **CONDITIONAL GO**

Ship the skill internally first. If dogfood proves it:

**Product wedge:** Idea to cited research to kill/go to repo scaffold to agent crew handoff.

Not "get a score." Sell execution unblocking for solo/AI-native founders.

| Track | What to sell | Price sketch |
|---|---|---|
| Micro | Cursor skill + templates (this repo) | $29-$79 one-time |
| Pro | Hosted pipeline + saved ventures + citations | $19-$39/mo |
| Team | Multi-venture workspace for studios | later |

## Why this can still make money before a big company

1. Dogfood to case studies (ventures actually shipped)
2. Sell the skill/kit on indie channels (micro mode of itself)
3. Only then consider hosted SaaS with live browsing + GitHub export

## Success metrics (prove it works)

- Time from idea to scaffold under 1 session
- At least 50% of GO verdicts get a first commit in 7 days
- At least one paying micro-product or waitlist conversion from a forged venture in 30 days
- Kill rate honesty: many ideas should die (if everything is GO, the skill is flattering you)

## Positioning under Shua Labs

- Shua Labs = holding / brand
- Startup Forge = venture factory skill (internal to optional product)
- Downstream products = the actual consumer/B2B companies

## Bottom line

Build the skill now. Use it to ship real products. Package later only if the wedge stays research to build, not another score.
